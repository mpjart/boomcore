The Boom Rule

When a die explodes, throw it out and roll two dice of the same size in its place. New dice can explode too. A chain of explosions is a boom.

DieExplodes onHow often
d2 to d6a 1d6: about 1 in 6
d7 to d20the minimum or maximumd8: 1 in 4 · d20: 1 in 10
d21 and largera flat 10% chance1 in 10

Example: d6 rolls 1 → roll 2d6: 1 and 4. Keep 4; the 1 → 2d6: 2 and 3. Total 4 + 2 + 3 = 9.

Boom cap: one roll never uses more than 100 dice. At 100, nothing else explodes.

0 stat points: roll a single d6 that does not explode. 1+ points: one d6 per point, and they explode.

Group roll: one roll for the party. No rerolls, passive checks or helping.

Falling in Battle

Level 2+: below 1 HP → lose a level, a stat point, and any ability over your slots (random).

Level 1: 0 HP → dies. Recommended: 24 hours to resurrect, else a new level 1 hero.

Turn Order

Initiative: d20 + Speed, highest first. You may hold your turn until later in the round, but you must act that round.

Movement: 5 + level + Speed spaces (1 space = 5 ft), before, after or split around actions.

Actions: 1, plus 1 per 4 Speed. Each costs one action: an ability, an attack (one hand or both), or using an object.

Conditions

Conditions never roll. T = the tier of what caused it, as a flat number: T damage · T rounds in combat · T hours outside it · T spaces (5 ft each). A T3 condition = 3 damage, 3 rounds, 3 hours, 3 spaces (15 ft).

Suggested defaults. No tier given? The Core Master picks one. The Core Master decides which conditions exist and can invent any.

Spirit Coins

1 spirit coin = 1 hour of labor = gruel to feed one person for a day.

Work caps at 10 hours (10 coins) a day, then 10 hours of rest. Heroes also roll their Spirit/Luck dice once a day for coins without working.
