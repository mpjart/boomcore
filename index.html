🎲 Boom Dice

Dice that hit their max or min explode 💥 How it works

Every die that lands on its max or min explodes (d6 and smaller only explode on a 1; d21 and larger explode on a flat 10% chance instead): it's discarded and 2 new dice are rolled. Everything left adds up.

Boom cap: one die group stops exploding after 100 dice in total (a d1 would explode forever, and a d2 can run very long).

Your rolls show in this color for the whole party. Rolls made from a character card use that character's color.
Open a loot box for coins, cores, and treasure 🎁 How it works

The Core Master makes loot boxes in the CM tab, and they wait here until they are opened. A box for one hero is opened by that hero's player (or the Core Master). A group box, for the whole party or the party stash, has a timer: everyone taps Grab it before time runs out, and it opens when the timer ends or when every hero has grabbed it. The feed shows who opened each box.

Inside: spirit coins, (monster level + hero level)d6 (at least 2d6), in the coin tier for the monster's level. Then a pool of d100s: a 1 explodes into 2 dice, a 100 upgrades the box a tier and explodes. Every other die is its own loot at the final tier. Even rolls also drop a soul core of that tier.

Ability tokens stack. Learning a new ability costs 1 token and a T1 core or better. Training an ability to a tier costs 1 token and a core of that tier or better, so an upgraded box's cores unlock stronger training.

A hero's box goes to their coin totals and Loot list. A whole-party box gives every hero a full copy, nothing split: use it only for bosses and for finishing a quest or dungeon. A stash box drops one copy in the party stash.
