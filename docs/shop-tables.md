# Boom Shop Tables

*Updated October 27, 2026. Shops for Core and Fantasy, Pirate, Steampunk, Horror, Street Grit, and Space. The [Boom Dice app](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/) sells from these lists while the market is open.*

## How Shopping Works

- **Every theme has the same six shops** with its own stock: clothes, general goods, tools, potions and remedies, travel, and plain weapons and armor. Use your table's theme, or mix shops from any theme.
- **Shops are open only while the market is open** (The Market, Chapter 6).
- **Every hero already has dimensional storage,** so nobody needs to shop for a bigger bag.
- **Prices are fixed,** in coins of the item's tier. A T2 riding horse at 3 costs 3 Uncommon coins (300 Common).
- **The price dial applies.** At +20%, a 10-coin potion costs 12. Round down.
- **Shop goods have no stats.** Rope is rope. Anything that adds to a stat or damage is loot, not shopping.
- **Weapons and armor are plain.** A shop weapon rolls the usual dice for its hands and reach (Chapter 3), with no plus, so a sword and a lead pipe hit the same; the look is flavor. Every weapon here is physical. Shop armor is always worth 1, physical or magical.
- **No sell-backs.** Shops buy loot, not their own stock. Once you've bought it, it's yours, and so is the buyer's remorse.
- **For scale:** 1 Common coin is an hour of work and a day of food. A peasant makes 8 to 10 a day.
