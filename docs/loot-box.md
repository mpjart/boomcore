# Boom Loot Box: Quick Reference

*Updated October 28, 2026. The whole loot box on one page, from Chapter 6 of the [Boom Core Guidelines](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/rules.html). The [Boom Dice app](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/) rolls all of it for you.*

## 1. Coins (Every Box)

Roll **(monster level + your level)d6**, at least 2d6 and at most 20d6. The dice explode on a 1. The coins are of the **monster's coin tier**, set by its level:

| Monster level | Coin tier | One coin is worth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 | Common | 1 spirit coin |
| 11-20 | Uncommon | 100 Common |
| 21-40 | Rare | 100 Uncommon |
| 41-60 | Epic | 100 Rare |
| 61-80 | Legendary | 100 Epic |
| 81-100 | Divine | 100 Legendary |

## 2. Box Tier

The **monster's rarity** sets the box: Common T1, Uncommon T2, Rare T3, Epic T4, Legendary T5, Divine T6. The Core Master can upgrade any box. The box tier sets item quality, the tier of every core, and what reagents and potions are worth. A lucky box is lucky.

## 3. The d100 Pool

Start with **1d100**. Dice never add; every die is its own pull. Roll the whole pool first, then resolve every die at the **highest tier the box reached**.

| d100 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Boom:** no loot, and the die becomes 2d100 |
| 2-50, even | A **sellable reagent** worth the number rolled, in coins of the box's tier |
| 3-49, odd | A **healing potion** of the box's tier |
| 51-70 | An **ability token** (it can also mimic this monster's signature ability once) |
| 71-99 | An **item** (see below) |
| 100 | **Upgrade:** no loot; the box goes up one tier (max T6) and the die becomes 2d100 |

**Any even roll from 2 to 98** also drops a **soul core** of the monster's level, at the box's final tier. A roll of 100 never drops a core. The pool stops growing at 100 dice.

## 4. Items

Roll a **d4** for the type: 1 weapon, 2 armor, 3 jewelry, 4 gem. From T2 up, roll a **d6** for the stat it boosts: 1 Constitution, 2 Strength, 3 Intellect, 4 Speed, 5 Charm, 6 Spirit/Luck. Gems never get a stat; they're treasure to sell.

| Box tier | Bonus | Armor per piece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Common | none | 1 |
| T2 Uncommon | +1 | 2 |
| T3 Rare | +2 | 3 |
| T4 Epic | +3 | 4 |
| T5 Legendary | +4 | 5 |
| T6 Divine | +5 | 6 |

A **+N weapon** rolls N extra copies of its dice: a +1 one-handed gun rolls 2d4. Name it with the [Loot Name Tables](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/loot-names.html): a clever name plus a base item, such as Black Hawk Longbow (+2 Speed).

## 5. Using Loot

- **Selling** happens only while the market is open. Reagents sell for their number; items, gems, potions, and cores (if the Core Master allows) sell for a d100 roll, which can boom, in coins of the box tier. The price dial applies.
- **Healing potions** take an action and heal one d6 per tier (a T3 potion heals 3d6).
- **Cores** fill the XP bar: 10 cores of your level make a level, or 1 core of a higher level fills the rest of the bar. Never more than one level per core.
- **Abilities:** a token plus a core of the right tier learns a new T1 ability or trains one up a tier.
- **Party loot:** for bosses and finishing a quest or dungeon, roll one box and every hero gets a full copy, using the party's average level for coins.

## 6. Example

Bob opens a T1 box from a level 3 monster and rolls a **1**: no loot, and now he has 2d100. He rolls **100** and **64**. The 100 upgrades the box to **T2** and becomes 2 more dice: **38** and **79**.

At T2: the **64** is an ability token plus a T2 core (even). The **38** is a reagent worth 38 Uncommon coins plus a T2 core. The **79** is a T2 item with +1 to a stat, rolled on a d6, and no core (odd). Plus his coins: (3 + Bob's level)d6 Common coins.
