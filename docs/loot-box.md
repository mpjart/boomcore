# Boom Loot Box: Quick Reference

*Updated October 28, 2026. The whole loot box on one page, from Chapter 6 of the [Boom Core Guidelines](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/rules.html). The [Boom Dice app](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/) rolls all of it for you. One kind of die does everything: the d100. The only extra rolls are booms.*

## 1. The Tier (No Roll)

The **monster's rarity** sets the box. The Core Master can upgrade any box.

| Box tier | Monster rarity | Item bonus | Armor per piece | Potion heals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Common | Everyday monsters | none | 1 | 1d6 |
| T2 Uncommon | Tougher | +1 | 2 | 2d6 |
| T3 Rare | Dangerous | +2 | 3 | 3d6 |
| T4 Epic | Bosses | +3 | 4 | 4d6 |
| T5 Legendary | Legends | +4 | 5 | 5d6 |
| T6 Divine | Gods | +5 | 6 | 6d6 |

The box tier sets items, potions, every core's tier, and what reagents are worth. A lucky box is lucky. **Coins** always use the **monster's coin tier**, set by its level: Common 1-10, Uncommon 11-20, Rare 21-40, Epic 41-60, Legendary 61-80, Divine 81-100 (each coin is worth 100 of the tier below).

## 2. The d100 Pool

Start with **1d100**. Dice never add; every die is its own pull. Roll the whole pool first, then read every die at the **highest tier the box reached**.

| d100 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Boom:** no loot, and the die becomes 2d100 |
| 2-21 | **Coins:** the number rolled, in the monster's coin tier |
| 22-35 | **Sellable reagent:** worth the number rolled, in coins of the box's tier |
| 36-50 | **Healing potion** of the box's tier |
| 51-70 | **Ability token** (it can also mimic this monster's signature ability once) |
| 71-78 | **Weapon** |
| 79-86 | **Armor** |
| 87-93 | **Jewelry** |
| 94-99 | **Gem** (treasure to sell, never a stat) |
| 100 | **Upgrade:** no loot; the box goes up one tier (max T6) and the die becomes 2d100 |

**Any even roll from 2 to 98** also drops a **soul core** of the monster's level, at the box's final tier. A roll of 100 never drops a core. The pool stops growing at 100 dice.

## 3. Read the Item (No Roll)

From T2 up, a weapon, armor, or jewelry gets the box's bonus on one stat. The **ones digit** of its roll picks the stat:

| Ones digit | Stat |
| --- | --- |
| 0, 1, 2 | Constitution |
| 3, 4 | Strength |
| 5, 6 | Intellect |
| 7 | Speed |
| 8 | Charm |
| 9 | Spirit/Luck |

**The number names it too:** a 74 is clever name #74 on the [Loot Name Tables](https://mpjart.github.io/boomcore/loot-names.html). The hero or Core Master picks a base item that fits, such as Black Hawk Longbow (+1 Strength). A gem uses the number to pick the gem, plus the tier's quality word. A **+N weapon** rolls N extra copies of its dice: a +1 one-handed gun rolls 2d4.

## 4. Using Loot

- **Selling** happens only while the market is open. Reagents sell for their number; items, gems, potions, and cores (if the Core Master allows) sell for a d100 roll, which can boom, in coins of the box tier. The price dial applies.
- **Healing potions** take an action and heal one d6 per tier (a T3 potion heals 3d6).
- **Cores** fill the XP bar: 10 cores of your level make a level, or 1 core of a higher level fills the rest of the bar. Never more than one level per core.
- **Abilities:** a token plus a core of the right tier learns a new T1 ability or trains one up a tier.
- **Party loot:** for bosses and finishing a quest or dungeon, roll one box and every hero gets a full copy.

## 5. Example

Bob opens a T1 box from a level 3 monster and rolls a **1**: no loot, and now he has 2d100. He rolls **100** and **64**. The 100 upgrades the box to **T2** and becomes 2 more dice: **18** and **74**.

At T2: the **64** is an ability token plus a T2 core (even). The **18** is 18 Common coins (a level 3 monster's coin tier) plus a T2 core. The **74** is a T2 weapon with +1 Strength (its ones digit is 4) plus a T2 core.
