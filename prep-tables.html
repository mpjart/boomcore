How to Use These Tables

  • Roll a d100 on any table. Mix themes freely: a Space rumor works fine in a Pirate port if you squint.
  • 01 is always Boom. Roll twice more and use both results together. Roll another 01 and it booms again.
  • No rerolls, same as everywhere else in Boom. If a result doesn't fit, twist it until it does, or just pick one.
  • Rumors are as true as the Core Master needs them to be. Not sure? Roll a d6: 1 or 2 it's false, 3 or 4 it's half true, 5 or 6 it's true.
  • Encounters get tougher as the roll goes up. The roll sets the monster's rarity, which sets its loot box (Chapter 6). Size the fight with Fair Encounters and build the monsters in the app's CM tab (Chapter 9). Rows marked No fight are scenes: someone to talk to, something strange, a choice to make.
  • Too tough for the party? Don't reroll. Turn it into a sighting, tracks, or a warning. They'll meet it later, when they're ready, or when they're not.
  • Plot hooks pair well with a rumor. Roll one of each and connect them.
  • Every creature is generic or a Boom original, like the sharksquid and the griffenchicken. Rename anything to fit your story.
